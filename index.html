UAV Propulsion Digital Twin & Intelligent PHM System Architecture

This system bridges conventional threshold-based UAV engine monitoring with Physics-Informed Edge AI (PINN) and hybrid thermodynamic data-driven models for autonomous predictive maintenance.

1. Physics-Informed AI (PINN) Core Formulation

Unlike pure black-box deep learning, our model embeds the 1st Law of Thermodynamics directly into the neural network loss function: Loss = MSE(actual, predicted) + λ · |Q_combustion - (W_shaft + Q_exhaust + Q_cooling + Q_friction)|. This prevents physically impossible predictions and ensures zero false-positives under altitude pressure changes.

2. Remaining Useful Life (RUL) Weibull Hazard Formulation

RUL is computed via a multi-stress cumulative damage accumulator combining:

  • Thermal Fatigue: Manson-Coffin plastic strain cycles calculated from per-cylinder CHT variations.
  • Vibration Energy: Cumulative tri-axial RMS acceleration integral over engine flight hours.
  • Combustion Shock: Misfire frequency and in-cylinder peak pressure spikes exceeding 90 bar.
  • Lubrication Degradation: Oil viscosity index deterioration under boundary lubrication regime.

3. Explainable AI (XAI) & Autonomous Maintenance Work-Orders

When an anomaly is flagged, the system produces real-time SHAP attribution scores revealing whether the root-cause is thermal, electrical, mechanical, or fuel-related, and immediately outputs autonomous maintenance work-orders.
